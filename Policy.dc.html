Policies

Studio policies

A few simple rules keep things fair and running smoothly for everyone. By booking an appointment, you agree to these policies.

Deposits

A 30% deposit is required to book every service. Your deposit is applied to your total, and the remaining balance is due at your appointment.

Cancellations & no-shows

Deposits are non-refundable for no-call/no-shows or cancellations made less than 12 hours before your appointment.

Need to change your time? Please reschedule or cancel at least 12 hours ahead.

Photo ID required

For liability purposes, all new clients must email a clear photo of a valid government-issued photo ID (such as a driver's license or state ID) before their first appointment.

Email it to YOUR-EMAIL-HERE with your full name and appointment date in the subject line. Your ID is kept private and used only to verify your identity.

Color services

New clients must book a Color Consultation before any color service. We'll talk about your goals, check your hair's health, and plan the best service for you.

Pricing

All prices are starting prices. Your final price depends on hair length, thickness, and style, and any add-ons are paid at your appointment.
