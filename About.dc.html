About

Hi, I'm Jay.

Fair warning: you will laugh in my chair. Probably a lot. I take your hair very seriously — myself, much less so. Good hair days should be fun, and I intend to keep it that way.

I'm new to Detroit, and honestly? I'm here to make friends. Every client who sits in my chair is a relationship I want to keep — I want to know your hair, your story, and what you did last weekend. The studio is in my home in Redford, it's brand new, and I'm upgrading it constantly. Come back in a month and I promise something will be nicer than it was.

What I care about most

Natural hair health. I specialize in keeping natural hair healthy — real treatments, honest trims, heat handled responsibly. My goal is that your hair is in better shape every time you leave than when you came in.

Caring for seniors. I spent over 10 years working with dementia patients, and it shaped how I work: patient, gentle, never rushed. If you're booking for a parent or grandparent, know that they'll be treated with exceptional care — in the studio or in their own home.
